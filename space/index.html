Hold the Disclosure: transcript replay

A customer asks a fictional bank's chatbot about an investment or loan, then pushes back for five turns (brevity, social proof, urgency, "skip the risks"). Does the reply at each turn still state every required disclosure? These are the 120 test2 dialogues (new pushback lines, English, Hindi and Hinglish) from the study.
